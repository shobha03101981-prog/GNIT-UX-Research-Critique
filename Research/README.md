# GNIT Website – UX Research

## Primary Research Question

How might we help GNIT students access academic information and institutional services easily, reliably, and consistently across mobile and desktop devices?

## Research Objectives

- Understand students' difficulties while navigating GNIT portals.
- Identify mobile usability and accessibility issues.
- Understand problems faced during important academic activities.
- Explore ways to improve navigation, feedback, and reliability.

## Assumptions to Validate

1. Students experience difficulty navigating between different GNIT portals.
2. Students face usability problems while accessing academic services on mobile devices.
3. Students need clearer feedback and better recovery options when institutional portals fail to load.

## Research Methods

- Student feedback and short interviews.
- Screenshot-based usability evaluation.
- Task-based website walkthroughs.
- Observation of navigation and portal behaviour.

## Research Status

Initial assumptions identified. Validation is pending.
